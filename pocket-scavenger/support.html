About Pocket Scavenger

Pocket Scavenger is a cozy wasteland survival game about scavenging supplies, managing your backpack, upgrading your shelter, trading with survivors, building relationships with NPCs, improving settlement reputation, and surviving one more night.

Common Questions

Pocket Scavenger does not require account registration or login. Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device.

If you experience an issue, please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened.

Response Time
