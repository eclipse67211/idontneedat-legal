Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Pocket Scavenger, a cozy wasteland survival game. We respect your privacy and keep the app simple.

1. Information We Collect

Pocket Scavenger does not require account registration or login.

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, payment information, or social media accounts.

2. Local Game Data

Game progress, settings, shelter progress, inventory progress, unlocked content, NPC relationship progress, settlement reputation, and other gameplay information may be stored locally on your device.

This local data is used only to make the game work and remember your progress.

3. No Account System

Pocket Scavenger does not use an external account system, social login, cloud account, or user profile system.

4. Purchases

If Pocket Scavenger is offered as a paid app or includes paid features, payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store.

We do not receive or store your credit card number, billing address, or other payment credentials.

5. Support Emails

If you contact us for support, we may use your email address only to respond to your request.

We do not sell or share support emails for advertising purposes.

6. Third-Party Services

Pocket Scavenger does not require third-party account login or social networking features.

Apple may process App Store purchases, downloads, crash reports, or app analytics according to Apple’s own privacy policies and settings.

7. Children’s Privacy

Pocket Scavenger is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not include user-generated content, public chat, or social networking features.

8. Data Security

Since gameplay information may be stored locally on your device, you are responsible for managing your device, backups, and app data.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be made available on this page.
