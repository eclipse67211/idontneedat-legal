Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

UA Companion is designed as a practical information companion for Ukrainians and displaced people looking for help with documents, benefits, healthcare, education, work, reminders, and trusted support resources.

Information We Collect

UA Companion does not require users to create an account. The app does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, payment information, or government identification numbers.

Information entered into the app, such as reminders, document notes, checklist progress, or saved items, is intended to stay on the user’s device unless a future version clearly states otherwise.

How We Use Information

Any information stored in the app is used only to provide app features such as checklists, reminders, saved resources, and basic personal organization.

Third-Party Services

The app may link to external websites such as government pages, humanitarian organizations, legal aid resources, healthcare services, education pages, and official support services. These external websites have their own privacy policies and practices.

UA Companion is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or availability of external websites.

Analytics and Tracking

UA Companion does not use third-party advertising tracking, cross-app tracking, or sell user data.

Children’s Privacy

UA Companion is intended for general informational use. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data. We do not share personal data with advertisers.

Data Security

We aim to keep the app simple and privacy-conscious by minimizing data collection. Users should avoid entering highly sensitive personal information unless necessary for their own personal notes.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
