Ovlap Wallet Privacy Policy

Ovlap Wallet is designed as a simple local wallet and card tracking utility. This page explains how information is handled.

Introduction

Ovlap Wallet helps users organize wallet and card-related information. The app is designed to be simple, practical, and privacy-conscious.

Information We Collect

Ovlap Wallet does not require users to create an account. Information entered into the app, such as card names, notes, balances, or settings, is used only to provide the app’s core features.

How We Use Information

User-entered information is used to display and manage wallet-related details inside the app. We do not sell personal data and do not use user data for advertising.

Data Storage

Ovlap Wallet is intended to store app data locally on the user’s device unless future features are added. If cloud sync, analytics, paid features, or other services are introduced, this policy may be updated.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data. We do not share user-entered wallet information with advertisers.

Children’s Privacy

Ovlap Wallet is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
