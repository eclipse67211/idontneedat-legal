About Loot Ledger

Loot Ledger is a simple IT asset and device inventory tracker for small teams. It helps users track assets, devices, accessories, assigned users, locations, notes, operations, status changes, and inventory activity.

Common Help Topics

  • Adding or editing assets
  • Tracking assigned users
  • Managing locations and asset status
  • Using operations and workflow tasks
  • Restoring Loot Ledger Pro purchases
  • Exporting or reviewing inventory records

Important Notice

Loot Ledger is a manual inventory tracking tool. It does not connect to Intune, Jamf, MDM systems, ticketing systems, accounting software, or company identity systems. All records are created and managed by the user.
