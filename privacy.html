Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

I Don’t Need a Therapist is a self-reflection and journaling app. The app is designed to help users write, reflect, and organize their own thoughts.

Information We Collect

The app does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or personal identity information.

Journal Content

Your journal entries and reflection content are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your journal content to our own server.

In-App Purchases

The app may offer in-app purchases. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment information.

Third-Party Services

The app may use RevenueCat to help manage in-app purchase access and restore purchases. RevenueCat may process purchase-related identifiers and transaction information needed to verify subscription or purchase status.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your journal content with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Journal content stays on your device unless you delete it or uninstall the app. You can delete your entries inside the app if that feature is available, or remove the app from your device to delete local app data.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.
