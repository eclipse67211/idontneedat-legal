Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 7, 2026

MaplePath is a personal planning and organization app designed to help users track timelines, planning notes, checklist items, and related preparation information.

Information We Collect

MaplePath does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Information entered into the app, such as planning notes, checklist items, timeline details, and evidence entries, is stored locally on the user's device unless otherwise stated in the app.

Purchases and Subscriptions

MaplePath offers optional Pro features through Apple In-App Purchase. Subscription purchase processing is handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

RevenueCat may process purchase-related information such as subscription status, product ID, transaction status, anonymous app user identifiers, and device/app information needed to manage subscription access.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide app functionality
  • Enable Pro subscription access
  • Restore previous purchases
  • Improve app reliability and user experience

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Purchase-related information may be processed by Apple and RevenueCat for subscription management and app functionality.

Legal, Immigration, Tax, and Financial Advice

MaplePath is a personal planning and organization tool only. It does not provide legal, immigration, tax, financial, or professional advice. Users should verify official requirements with government sources or consult a qualified professional.

Children's Privacy

MaplePath is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Retention

Local app data remains on the user's device unless deleted by the user or removed when the app is uninstalled. Purchase records may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their own policies.
