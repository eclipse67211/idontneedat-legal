Common Questions

Do I need an account?

No. MaplePath can be used without creating an account or signing in.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open the Pro page in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Does MaplePath provide immigration advice?

No. MaplePath is a personal planning and organization tool only. It does not provide legal, immigration, tax, financial, or professional advice.

Where can I manage my subscription?

You can manage or cancel subscriptions from your Apple Account settings on your iPhone.
