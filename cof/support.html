When Contacting Support

To help us understand the issue faster, please include:

  • Your device model
  • Your iOS version
  • A short description of the problem
  • Screenshots or screen recordings, if available

Common Issues

The game is not opening or crashes

Please make sure your iPhone or iPad is updated to the latest available iOS version. Restarting the device may also help.

Progress or coins look incorrect

Please describe what happened before the issue occurred. Include a screenshot if possible.

Purchase issue

App Store purchases are processed by Apple. If a purchase does not appear correctly, please contact us with the details and also check your Apple purchase history.

We welcome feedback about gameplay balance, upgrades, customers, cafe visuals, and future improvements.
