Common Questions

Is Ovlap Wallet financial advice?

No. Ovlap Wallet is a personal organization and reward estimation tool. It does not provide financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple Account settings. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your Apple Account, then tap Subscriptions.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the app, go to the Pro or Upgrade screen, and tap Restore Purchases.
