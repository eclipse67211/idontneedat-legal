Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Ovlap Wallet is a manual card tracking app. It is designed to help you organize card-related information without linking a bank account.

The app does not require an account, does not require bank linking, and does not ask for online banking login details.

1. Information You Enter

Ovlap Wallet may allow you to manually enter information such as:

  • Card names or custom card labels
  • Balances
  • Spending notes
  • Currency symbols
  • Custom card details or wallet activity you choose to record

This information is created and managed by you for personal tracking purposes.

2. Local Storage

Ovlap Wallet is designed as a local manual tracking tool. Information you enter may be stored locally on your device.

Ovlap Wallet does not operate a server to collect, view, or manage your manually entered card tracking data.

3. No Bank Linking

Ovlap Wallet does not connect to banks, credit card issuers, payment networks, financial institutions, or financial data providers.

The app does not ask for online banking usernames, banking passwords, security answers, or other bank login credentials.

4. Sensitive Financial Information

Do not enter full card numbers, CVV codes, PINs, banking passwords, online banking usernames, security answers, or other sensitive financial credentials into Ovlap Wallet.

Ovlap Wallet is intended for manual organization and tracking only, not for storing sensitive financial credentials.

5. Data Collection

Ovlap Wallet does not collect your manually entered card tracking data on a developer-operated server.

The app does not sell your personal data.

6. App Store Purchase Processing

Ovlap Wallet is distributed through the Apple App Store. App download, payment, refund, and purchase-related processing are handled by Apple.

Apple may process information according to Apple’s own privacy policies and App Store terms.

7. Third-Party Services

Ovlap Wallet does not require third-party financial account linking or banking data services.

If future versions of the app include analytics, crash reporting, cloud backup, or other third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those services.

8. Data Sharing

Ovlap Wallet does not sell your data.

Because the app is designed for local manual tracking, the developer does not access or share the card tracking information you enter into the app.

9. Data Backup and Device Responsibility

Your app data may depend on your device storage and backup settings. You are responsible for managing your device, backups, app data, and any information you choose to enter.

Deleting the app or clearing app data may remove locally stored information.

10. Children’s Privacy

Ovlap Wallet is not intended for children. The app is designed as a personal organization tool for users who manage their own card-related information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be made available on this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.
