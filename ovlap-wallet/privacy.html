Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Ovlap Wallet is a personal wallet and reward organization app. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Information We Collect

Ovlap Wallet does not require users to create an account. The app may allow users to enter card names, reward categories, spending caps, and related wallet information for personal organization and reward comparison.

Local Storage

Information entered into the app is stored locally on the user’s device unless the user chooses to export, back up, or otherwise share it outside the app.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Ovlap Wallet uses Apple In-App Purchase for subscriptions. Subscription purchase and billing information is handled by Apple. We do not collect or store users’ payment card numbers.

Third-Party Services

The app may use RevenueCat to help manage subscription status and entitlement access. RevenueCat may process limited purchase-related identifiers needed to verify subscription status.

Data Selling

We do not sell personal data.

Analytics and Advertising

Ovlap Wallet does not use third-party advertising. If analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated to explain what is collected and why.

Financial Disclaimer

Ovlap Wallet is for personal organization and reward estimation only. It does not provide financial, tax, legal, or investment advice. Reward rates, offers, and card terms may change. Users should confirm details directly with their card issuer.
