Common Questions

How do I use the App?

Enter your bill details, select relevant categories, and use the App to review cost breakdowns, compare expenses, run what-if scenarios, and save reports for personal reference.

How do I unlock Pro features?

Pro features can be unlocked through an optional auto-renewable subscription using Apple In-App Purchase. Open the Pro screen in the App to view available subscription options.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. The App will check your Apple account and restore Pro access if an active subscription is found.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID account settings. Deleting the App does not automatically cancel an active subscription.
